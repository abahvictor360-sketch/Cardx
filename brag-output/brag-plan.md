# Cardx — brag plan

## Rubric

- **What is it?** Cardx is a modern banking app and card: a metal card, instant virtual cards and smart budgeting that works in 150+ currencies.
- **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?** For people who spend, save and get paid across borders. One account and card shows where every dollar goes and charges no fees abroad.
- **What sets it apart?** It looks and feels like hardware: chrome, steel and a single orange accent. Its claims are concrete: no foreign transaction fees, 150+ currencies, and spending cut by 32% on average.
- **Most impressive claim:** "32% average spending reduction", together with "Zero fees abroad."
- **Visual hook:** the chrome robotic claw from the hero, slamming down with the black Cardx card in its grip.
- **Real UI to show:** the hero (headline, claw and reviews row), the bank-card component, the "Use it anywhere in the world" promo card, the scrolling feature ticker, the October budget panel with the 32% stat, the Metal/Virtual/Business card trio and the orange Sign Up button.
- **Tone:** `polished`, pushed toward `app-store` energy. The brand is serious fintech, so there is no parody. The pace stays punchy, with the cuts landing on the beat.
- **Share caption:** "Built Cardx: a metal card, instant virtual cards and budgeting that actually bites — 150+ currencies, zero fees abroad."

## Angle

The **claw is the brand.** The video opens on the hero's claw dropping onto the beat and gripping the card. From then on, every scene is something Cardx "gets a grip on": the card, the world, your budget and your plan. It ends with "Your money, finally under control." That line is the site's own CTA copy, and it pays off the claw.

## Visual identity (from the source)

- **Font:** Inter Tight, with display weight 450 and tight tracking (−0.055em).
- **Colours:** ink `#0b0b0b`, chrome radial greys `#ececec → #b9b9b9`, white, and orange `#f25c1f` as the single accent.
- **Assets reused directly:** `assets/css/styles.css` and its components (`.bank-card`, `.promo`, `.panel` and `.bar-row`, `.ticker`, `.avatars`, `.btn`), `claw.svg` (with the bob animation stripped so every frame is a pure function of time), the people photos, and the logo SVG.

## Sound

The music is original and synthesised for this video: 120 BPM in A minor, with the chords Am–F–C–G twice, then F–C to finish bright. It uses a warm additive pad (sidechained to the kick), an eighth-note bass, four-on-the-floor kick from 3.0s, claps on 2 and 4, offbeat hats, and a quiet pluck arpeggio in the highlights. The SFX are tuned to the key (A and E) and share the music's reverb, so they sit under the music rather than on top of it. The mix is soft-clipped and normalised to −1 dBFS.
